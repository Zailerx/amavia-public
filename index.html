Privacy policy

Updated: 7 October 2026

Amavia is a private space for adult couples to plan dates, answer relationship questions and share wishes and memories. Its data controller is Viktoras Sirvidas Daunoravicius, Spain. Contact: oleksandrasylchenko@gmail.com.

Information we process

Account identifiers, name and email; optional date of birth, partner nickname, relationship start date and language; pairing codes and partner connection; your calendar plans, answers, wishes, saved date ideas and uploaded photos. The service also processes authentication and security records, and a device notification token when you enable supported notifications. Support receives information you choose to send.

You select photos yourself. Amavia does not upload your entire photo library or collect your precise location. Do not put sensitive information about yourself or another person in optional answers unless you want it processed and shared as described here.

Purposes and legal bases

We process information needed to provide the service you request, including accounts, synchronization and partner sharing, on the basis of performing our contract with you. Security and abuse prevention rely on our legitimate interest in protecting users and the service. Optional device permissions are requested before use and can be withdrawn in system settings. Where processing requires consent, you may withdraw it by contacting support. We also process information where necessary to meet legal obligations.

Partner sharing

After you link accounts, your partner can see the shared content allowed by each feature. Some answers remain hidden until the required answers are submitted. Your birthday's month and day may appear in the shared calendar; your birth year and account email are not shown to your partner through this feature. Disconnecting stops access to the shared space. Your partner may already have seen or retained shared content.

Providers and transfers

Supabase provides authentication, database and photo storage. Apple and Google provide distribution, optional sign-in and platform notification services when configured. Google handles email sent to our Gmail support address. These providers may process information outside your country; where required, transfers use applicable adequacy decisions or contractual safeguards. You can contact us for details. Provider processing is also described in their privacy notices: Supabase, Google and Apple.

The current app has no advertising, paid subscriptions or third-party analytics enabled. We do not sell your personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Account and app content are kept while needed to provide your active account. When you delete your account, associated app data is removed from the active service and stored photos are scheduled for removal. Shared events you created and related memories may be removed for both partners; the partner's own account is not deleted. Limited security, abuse-report and support records may be retained as necessary to resolve incidents or comply with legal obligations. Backup copies follow the hosting provider's retention cycle and are not used to restore a deleted account. Contact support for information about a particular request.

Use the account deletion instructions below. Uninstalling Amavia does not delete a server account.

Security

Amavia uses encrypted network connections, account authentication and access rules to restrict private content to authorized users. It is not an end-to-end encrypted service; authorized administrators may access information when necessary for operation, support or safety.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing where applicable, and withdraw consent. Write to the contact above; we may need to verify that you own the account. You may complain to the Spanish data protection authority, AEPD, or your local supervisory authority.

Amavia is intended for people aged 18 and over. Contact us if a child has supplied personal information. We may update this policy as the service changes and will communicate material changes through the app or the contact details on your account.

Delete your Amavia account and data

You can delete your account in Amavia: Profile → Configuration → Account security → Delete account, then confirm.

If you no longer have the app, send a request from the email used for your Amavia account to oleksandrasylchenko@gmail.com, with subject Delete my Amavia account. State that you want your account and associated data deleted. Never send your password or a sign-in code.

We may verify account ownership before processing the request. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days and will tell you if more information is required. Deletion covers the account, profile, personal answers, saved wishes and ideas, uploaded photos and events you created. Relevant shared event memories may also disappear for your partner. The partner's separate account remains. Any necessary retained security, abuse or legal records are limited to their purpose as described in the policy above.
